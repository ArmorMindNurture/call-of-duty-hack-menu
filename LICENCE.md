## 🧩 About

**Call of Duty Companion** is a curated list of Call of Duty open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, overlay notes, map overview
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — loadout catalog, skin manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — practice layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Practice Helpers
- Loadout Notes — record and review custom loadouts
- Hitbox Viewer — visualize weapon and player hitboxes
- Replay Notes — record and review match runs
- Playback Speed — adjust replay playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Skin Catalog — browse weapon skins, camos, and reticles
- Custom Loadout Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Visibility Notes — improve visibility of map elements

### 🏋️ Practice Layout
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study on any map
- Gravity Notes — manual notes on movement mechanics
- Jump Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in practice
